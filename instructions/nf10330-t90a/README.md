# NF10330 – NifeliZ T-90A main battle tank

Catalog entry `nf10330` (key `t90a`, `?anleitung=nf10330`). Booklet steps 1–65: the hull (steps 1–17; step 13 joins the
sub-assembly of steps 8–12), the two side beams (steps 18–50, set onto the hull in step 51) and the rear deck
(steps 52–64, set onto the beams in step 65). Sources: the booklet PDFs and the parts list
`NF10330-Nifeliz-T-90A-Teileliste.csv` (LDraw numbers) in `_source/`.

General rules (coordinates, element fields, animation rhythm, checks): `docs/PROJECT-RULES.md`.

## Files

```
model.json            all elements (generated by npm run model:t90a – never edit by hand)
expected-parts.json   parts box per booklet step (test reference)
checks.json           exceptions of this model for tools/check/entry-paths.mjs
model.test.ts         validation, overlaps, parts per step, entry ways (run by npm test)
build/gen-beams.py    writes all of model.json: hull steps 1-17 (list HULL), beams 18-50, deck (list DECK); writes beam50-map.json
build/rotate-beam.mjs ALL hand corrections of the beams and the deck and ALL animation distances
build/beam50-map.json pairs of beam 34 / beam 50 (generated)
```

```
npm run model:t90a                                  # gen-beams.py (Python 3), then rotate-beam.mjs
npm test                                            # includes model.test.ts of this instruction
npm run check:paths -- instructions/nf10330-t90a    # entry ways; LIFT=deck:7 tries another stage height
```

## Where a correction goes

- **Steps 1–17 (hull):** the list `HULL` in `build/gen-beams.py`: one line per part, `(step, part, lx, row)`. `lx` is the
  position along the hull (y, counted from the front edge `HY0 = -19`), `row` names a line of the hull in `ROWS` (x of the
  minimum corner, height z in plates, rotation). Start distances (`ENTRY`) and heights of the intermediate steps
  (`HOVER`) of these steps are set in `build/rotate-beam.mjs`.
- **Steps 18–51 (beams):** every correction into `build/rotate-beam.mjs` (`fix('sNN-MM', e => …)`, step blocks).
- **Beam 50 (steps 35–50) is the mirror image of beam 34 (steps 18–34)**, mirrored across the hull middle x = 6
  (`beam50-map.json` pairs the elements). Correct beam 34 and the mirror follows; corrections for beam 50 only go into the
  block "Corrections made by hand on beam 50" after the mirror. Sideways directions are swapped (+x ↔ -x), y and z stay.
- **Steps 52–64 (rear deck, bag 1):** `DECK` in `build/gen-beams.py` holds the first layout; `rotate-beam.mjs` turns the
  whole deck 180 degrees about the middle of the plate 4x12, moves it 2 studs to the back and holds ALL later corrections
  (block "Steps 52-64") – put new ones there.
- After each change: `npm run model:t90a`, `npm test`, `npm run build`.

## Geometry

- Hull: 12 studs wide (x 0–12), top of its plates z 2. Step 1: two plates 4x12 (the only positions marked `measured`) and
  two tiles 2x3; the first plate stands in its place from the start.
- The beams sit on top of the hull (+3 plates), one stud inward (asked; they overlap the hull grid in the test on purpose).
- Rear deck, final place: plate 4x12 across the hull on the top plates of the beams (x 0–12, y 6–10, z 7); the round-plate
  stacks of step 54 stand in the gap between the tall bricks of step 4.

## Staged sub-assemblies

| stage | steps | built above its place | join step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sub` | 8–12 | 3 studs | 13 |
| `beams` | 18–50 | 6 studs | 51 |
| `deck` | 52–64 | 8 studs | 65 |

The heights are the lowest at which nothing arriving from below reaches into the model. For `deck` a part from below must
also start above the top of the model around it (beam tops, z 7), even where the cells right under it are empty
(`topCheckStages` in `checks.json`).

## Animation exceptions (asked)

- Step 17: the side slopes are pushed in from the front (-y) and are fully visible at 2 studs.
- Step 30/46: axle 6 studs. Steps 31/32 (47/48): all parts start at one common point, so each way is shorter than the last.
  Step 33/49: 2.5 + 2.5. Step 34/50: axle 2, joiner 3.5, connector 4.

## Parts

Parts list of the booklet with LDraw numbers: `_source/NF10330-Nifeliz-T-90A-Teileliste.csv`; the parts are in
`parts/bricks/map.mjs`. Notes:

- 60476 in an early list is a clip brick in LDraw; the plate 1x12 is 60479.
- The right wing plate and block are the same LDraw part as the left one (mirrored / rotated); the slopes (60477) lie on
  their side.
- The wedge tiles 2x3 of step 3 use the wedge plates 43722a / 43723a without studs.
- Rear deck parts off the stud grid use the converter options `lie`, `spin` (bracket 1x2 on its side, standing plate 1x2,
  clip tiles of step 55), `tilt` + `tiltRebase` + `nudge` (shields of step 58, tilted 45°), `yaw` (bar with clip of step 62)
  and `_h` variants with `xShift: 0.5` (stack of step 62 on the single centre stud of the plate 1x2 with one stud).

## Known approximations

Parts stuck on sideways or turned off the grid share a grid cell with their neighbour. The known cases are listed in
`model.test.ts` (overlaps) and `checks.json` (entry ways): wedge tile of step 3 under the wing plates of step 14, axles
through Technic bricks (steps 30/46), the light clip plates of steps 19/36, beams reaching into the hull while built
apart, the side-mounted parts of steps 55/58 and the brackets 1x1 of step 63 beside the stack of step 62.

## Open points

- Positions apart from the plates of step 1 are estimates (`confidence: "estimate"`) and are corrected step by step
  against the booklet; this includes the layout inside the beams and their hardware (axles, discs, gold parts, pins).
- Special parts of steps 3–5 and 14–17 still to be confirmed; the parts of steps 14–17 are brown, only their side studs
  are black as in the booklet.
- Step 8: the plate 1x12 is in the parts box but not visible in the drawing.
